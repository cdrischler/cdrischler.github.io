Below is an overview of the courses I have taught at Ohio University, along with brief descriptions. A condensed list also appears on my [CV page](/cv/).

## PHYS 2002: Introduction to Physics (with lab)

**Semesters taught:** Spring 2026, Fall 2026

This is the second course in the introductory physics sequence and is open to students from all areas. A background in algebra, trigonometry, and geometry is required; calculus is not necessary. Topics include electricity, magnetism, waves, sound, light, relativity, quantum physics, atomic physics, and nuclear physics. The course carries 4 semester credit hours, with 3 hours of lecture and 2 hours of laboratory each week.

## PHYS 4071/5071: Computer Simulation Methods in Physics

**Semesters taught:** Fall 2023, Fall 2024, Fall 2025

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to numerical methods and computational tools for solving physics problems using the `Python` programming language. Students are introduced to numerical methods and to the computational approach to scientific problems. Topics covered include:

* numerical differentiation and integration methods,
* numerical error analysis,
* numerical linear algebra,
* data fitting,
* Monte Carlo strategies, and
* parallel computing.

## PHYS 6900: Computational Physics Summer Tutorials

**Semesters taught:** Summer 2023, Summer 2024, Summer 2025

A series of hands-on summer tutorials in computational physics. Materials are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/cdrischler/compphysics-summer-tutorials).

## PHYS 8501: Research Seminar in Nuclear and Particle Physics and Journal Club

**Semesters taught:** Spring 2023, Spring 2025

A research seminar and journal club for graduate students in nuclear and particle physics, covering current literature and ongoing research in the field.
